# Dashboard card references

- Official base rulebook: https://media.wizards.com/downloads/dnd/DnD_LOW_Rulebook_EN.pdf
- Official expansion rulebook and clarifications: https://media.wizards.com/downloads/dnd/SOS_Rulebook.pdf
- Factual quest names, types, printed VP, and expansion membership cross-referenced from Dave Choi's manually entered card reference: https://github.com/Dave-Choi/waterdeep-reference (app/models/quest.js and building.js).

The catalog contains factual metadata and short, rewritten reminders, not artwork, flavor text, or a reproduction of the complete cards. All rewards can be adjusted before recording; consult the physical card for choices and resource rewards. Resources, Intrigue cards, and building visits are not tracked.

Initial automated effects: the five +2 VP quest-type Plot Quests; Root Out Loviatar's Faithful (15 VP +2 per white cube, up to four flexible cubes); Establish Harpers Safe House (+2 per controlled building at completion); Unleash Crime Spree and Destroy a Temple of Selvetarm (optional +10); immediate Heroes' Garden completion (+4).

Infiltrate Builder’s Hall awards +4 VP automatically on purchases, including purchases with manually entered VP gems. Intrigue-playing bonuses and building visits stay in Additional points. Gem counts are entered manually when putting a building in play; transfers do not award gems. Start-of-round building effects only apply while the building is in play. Monsters Made to Order does not gain a skull if the track is empty. The round-five Agent instruction appears only when entering Round 5.

Corrections made to the reference: The Zoarstar, Hall of Three Lords, Shradin's Excellent Zombies use official rulebook names. The Poisoned Quill grants its owner an Intrigue card rather than Gold (no owner resource benefit is automated). Threaten the Builders' Guilds has an immediate building reward; no repeating scoring effect is assumed.

Lord counts are prefilled from named completions and current ownership only. Manual point entries do not establish a completed quest. Counts remain editable to reconcile missing entries or card effects. Mandatory Quests stay in manual point entry and do not count for Lords.

Purchasing a building with stocking instructions opens a setup reminder; this does not track resources or add the stocked VP gems to the owner’s score. The Palace’s Ambassador instruction appears in general round upkeep while the Palace is in play. The expansion rulebook limits ownership to nine buildings and provides no general voluntary destruction rule to exceed that limit.
